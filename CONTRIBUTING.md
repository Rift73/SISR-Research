# Contributing useful research, not just more text

Corrections, counterexamples and well-controlled experiments are welcome.
Start with the [research card](templates/research-card.md).

For a correction, identify the exact claim, provide the primary source or a
reproducible counterexample, and explain which conclusion changes. Distinguish a
source/math error from a design preference. A negative result is valuable when
the tested object and its limits are clear.

For a new experiment, include its contract, a fair baseline, retained failures,
raw measurements when safe, and a conclusion no broader than the evidence.
Do not submit a new “best” architecture based solely on operation counts, one
early validation point or an unpaired timing from another setup.

Keep each case focused on a decision. Prefer a short update to the governing
claim over duplicating a long discussion. Mark superseded claims visibly and
date meaningful status changes; do not silently rewrite history.

Do not upload secrets, private paths, datasets, checkpoints, copyrighted image
crops or third-party code without the necessary rights. Original documentation
contributions are accepted under the repository's CC BY 4.0 license; third-party
material must be clearly identified and separately licensed.
